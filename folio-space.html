THE IDEA ✳

Keep your thoughts close. Take them with you.

Folio Space brings notes, tasks, collections, and ideas into one simple workspace. It is designed to be useful without asking you to create an account or send everyday writing to a server.

When you need a page on another computer, create an encrypted online snapshot and import it with the same passkey. The snapshot remains online until deleted, and importing a shared page adds it to the destination workspace instead of replacing all its content.

Online sharing requires the Chord site to be deployed with its included Cloudflare Pages Functions and the NOTEFUL_TRANSFERS KV binding configured.
