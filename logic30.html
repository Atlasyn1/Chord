THE IDEA ✳

Think before you code.

Logic30 is designed around a simple belief: problem-solving gets stronger when you practise it regularly. Instead of jumping straight into complicated code, learners start by understanding the problem, identifying inputs and outputs, and breaking the solution into logical decisions.

The 30-day roadmap creates a clear path from fundamentals to more confident reasoning. Progress indicators and a day-by-day challenge help learners keep their momentum visible.
